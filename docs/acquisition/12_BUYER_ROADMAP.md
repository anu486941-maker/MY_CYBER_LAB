# 12 — Product Roadmap for Buyer

This document outlines an actionable post-acquisition product and commercial expansion roadmap for the new owner of CyberForge AI.

---

## Phase 1: Days 0 – 30 (Launch & Monetization Activation)

- **Domain & Branding Finalization**: Configure production custom domain and brand identity.
- **Payment Gateway Integration**: Connect Stripe or LemonSqueezy to unlock paid Pro subscriptions and one-time certification fees.
- **SEO & Organic Acquisition**: Index all public curriculum routes, cheat sheets, and the certificate verification portal.
- **Social & Community Launch**: Distribute demo clips showcasing AMAN voice tutoring and interactive cyber labs across Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Reddit (`/r/cybersecurity`, `/r/netsecstudents`).

---

## Phase 2: Days 30 – 90 (Community & Retention Optimization)

- **Discord / Community Integration**: Add webhook notifications for student milestones, streak celebrations, and CTF flag captures.
- **Enhanced Challenge Content**: Expand the CTF challenge database with additional forensic PCAP files and web vulnerabilities.
- **Expanded Video Library**: Curate additional high-quality multilingual video modules for advanced topics.
- **User Analytics & Telemetry**: Deploy privacy-compliant PostHog or Mixpanel event tracking to measure module drop-off rates and optimize lesson flow.

---

## Phase 3: Months 3 – 6 (B2B & Academic Capabilities)

- **Instructor & Team Lead Dashboard**: Build multi-seat management allowing team managers to assign specific modules, track team completion percentages, and review evidence.
- **SSO / SAML Authentication**: Enable Google Workspace, Okta, and Microsoft Azure AD single sign-on for corporate accounts.
- **Custom Certification Tracks**: Partner with regional training providers to issue co-branded digital credentials.

---

## Phase 4: Months 6 – 12 (Advanced AI & Cloud Integrations)

- **Dockerized Ephemeral VM Support (Optional)**: Introduce opt-in containerized backend targets for advanced kernel exploit labs.
- **Voice AI Enhancement**: Implement real-time WebRTC audio streaming for frictionless two-way voice conversations with AMAN.
- **Automated Incident Response Grader**: Upgrade AMAN's evaluation engine to automatically grade and provide detailed scoring rubrics for full-length penetration test writeups.

# 17 — Buyer Demo Script (5-Minute Walkthrough)

This script provides a concise 5-minute product demonstration designed for buyer due-diligence calls and screen shares.

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Introduction & Value Proposition
- **Action**: Share screen showing the CyberForge AI Command Center Dashboard (`/dashboard`).
- **Talking Points**:
  > "Welcome. Today I'm walking you through CyberForge AI—a production-ready cybersecurity training platform and AI cyber range. Our core mission is 'Action Over Theory': taking students from basic concepts to hands-on command-line operations with the help of AMAN, our embedded Socratic AI mentor."

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Onboarding & Role Calibration
- **Action**: Click profile or navigate to `/onboarding` / `/roles`.
- **Talking Points**:
  > "When a student arrives, they complete an interactive 8-step calibration. They select a target role—like SOC Analyst or Penetration Tester—set their study schedule, and take a quick baseline diagnostic test. The system automatically structures their entire learning roadmap based on their starting skill level."

---

### [1:15 – 2:30] Interactive Cyber Labs (Linux, Network, SOC)
- **Action**: Open the Linux Lab (`/linux-lab`), then open the Network Lab (`/network-lab`).
- **Talking Points**:
  > "Let's look at our practical labs. Here in the Linux Lab, we have a fully interactive in-browser CLI. Students run real commands—`ls`, `cat`, `chmod`, `grep`—to complete tactical objectives. Notice how fast it launches: no heavy virtual machines, no VPNs.
  > Over in the Network Lab, we have an interactive topology visualizer and subnet trainer where students calculate CIDR ranges and analyze packet flows between routers and servers in real time."

---

### [2:30 – 3:30] AMAN AI Mentorship & Socratic Guidance
- **Action**: Open the AI Mentor interface (`/ai-mentor`) or trigger the Ask AMAN drawer.
- **Talking Points**:
  > "Now, let's look at our major differentiator: AMAN. AMAN isn't a generic chatbot. It operates in 9 distinct pedagogical modes like `TEACH`, `DEBUG`, and `QUIZ`. If a student makes a syntax error in Nmap, AMAN pinpoints the error and explains the underlying concept using Socratic hints instead of simply giving away the answer. We also support full voice interaction, session archiving, and export to Markdown."

---

### [3:30 – 4:15] Progress, Evidence & Verified Certificates
- **Action**: Navigate to Evidence Locker (`/evidence`) and Certificates (`/certificate`).
- **Talking Points**:
  > "As students complete labs, their artifacts and flags are preserved in the Evidence Locker. When they finish a career milestone, the system issues a tamper-evident certificate with a unique verification hash that can be independently verified on our public `/verify-certificate` portal."

---

### [4:15 – 5:00] Architecture, Reliability & Commercial Opportunity
- **Action**: Bring up `⌘K` command palette and highlight the clean test/build status.
- **Talking Points**:
  > "Under the hood, CyberForge AI is built on React 19, TypeScript, Node.js, and Firebase. It features a complete automated test suite with 362 passing tests and zero TypeScript errors. It runs on free AI tiers and serverless storage, giving it a 90%+ gross margin profile. For an acquirer, this provides a complete, scalable EdTech asset ready for immediate monetization."

## 2. Value Analysis by Buyer Archetype

### A. Cybersecurity Bootcamps & Training Academies
- **Immediate Value**: Provides students with a 24/7 AI teaching assistant and automated lab environments between live lecture sessions.
- **Cost Reduction**: Reduces instructor grading overhead and live Q&A burden.
- **Scalability**: Onboard hundreds of simultaneous students without provisioning dedicated VM server farms.

### B. EdTech Operators & SaaS Aggregators
- **Immediate Value**: Turnkey SaaS asset ready for immediate B2C monetization via monthly subscriptions or one-time lifetime passes.
- **Low Operating Costs**: Operates on Gemini Free Tier and serverless Firebase architecture, ensuring high gross margins.
- **High Retention Mechanics**: Daily streaks, XP, modular missions, and progressive badges foster continuous engagement.

### C. Corporate Security Awareness & MSSP Vendors
- **Immediate Value**: A ready-made platform for training junior IT staff and Tier-1 SOC recruits on real triage workflows.
- **Client Engagement**: Can be white-labeled and offered to enterprise clients as a branded security upskilling perk.

### D. Solo Founders / Indie Hackers
- **Immediate Value**: Eliminates the high upfront engineering risk and enables the founder to focus 100% on product marketing, community building, and distribution.
- **Extensible Codebase**: Standard React 19, TypeScript, and Express stack allows straightforward feature additions and customization.
